# Surface brief: homepage (`/`, `/en/`)

## Scope and mode

Persuade. Replaces the old editorial "Deja una huella" homepage, whose web-studio content moves to `/webs/` and `/en/websites/`.

## Audience, job, action

- Operating businesses that want their own system, automations or AI, and founders who want to launch a SaaS product. Both need a clear path.
- Job: understand in seconds that Thexify designs and builds software with AI (built with Claude), trust a small team, and start a conversation.
- Primary action: "Cuéntanos tu idea" (contact). Secondary: see the solutions; websites live on their own page.
- Proof on hand: the "Somos Thexify" statement, the three published commitments, two website cases. No software cases exist; demonstration interfaces must read as examples, never as Thexify's own customers or metrics.

## Approved comps

- Hero: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-b-dividida.png` (approved 2026-10-11).
- Soluciones section: bento grid from `.impeccable/mocks/comp-c-bento.png`.
- Do not literalize: the uppercase label above the headline (eyebrow ban), the `thexify` logo inside the demo app, demo revenue figures, the "Stark Industries" style names.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Thexify's software is shown working, not described: real-looking product interfaces float in depth while two emerald beams cross behind them, the brand's two-direction X drawn as light. It refuses the centered gradient-blob SaaS hero and stock "AI" imagery.

OWN-WORLD: Near-black ink ground with film grain; off-white Manrope at heavy weight and tight tracking; emerald `#2FD79B` only on the primary action, active states and the light; interface cards in deep green-black glass with hairline borders, soft offset shadows and 3D perspective.

STORY: The visitor reads the promise, sees a dashboard, an automation and a Claude assistant already doing their jobs, learns the three solutions, trusts the commitments and process, then writes.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav across the top. Left 45%: three-line headline at display scale, paragraph, emerald "Cuéntanos tu idea" button plus "Ver soluciones" link, then "Construido con Claude de Anthropic". Right 55%: tilted stack (orders dashboard back, automation flow middle, Claude assistant front) over the crossing beams.

FORM: Category canon pinned by the founder (Vercel and Stripe craft bar), translated into the Thexify identity; outside the ranked list; seed 1f2eb10f.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
